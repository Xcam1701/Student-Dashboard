Notes & study tracker

Stays in sync across your devices

Nothing connected yet. The most reliable way to get a tracker that's genuinely synced across your phone and laptop, without building a database yourself, is to point this panel at a tool that already syncs: a Notion page, Google Tasks, or Todoist.

Open the "Personalize this dashboard" section below for the two-line change that embeds it here.

Personalize this dashboard
  1. Calendar: get your Google Calendar's embed link (Calendar settings → your calendar → "Integrate calendar" → Embed code) and replace the iframe src in the "This week" section.
  2. Notes: create a Notion page for your task list, click Share → "Share to web", copy the link, and replace the empty-state block in the "Notes & study tracker" section with an <iframe> pointing at that link. Prefer Google Tasks or Todoist instead? Same idea — most let you generate a public or embeddable view.
  3. Links: edit the href values marked edit link to point at your actual university portal, VLE and library.
  4. Term dates: replace "edit me" in the title block with your real term start date.
